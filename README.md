## 💫 About Me

I'm a software engineer who enjoys the unglamorous, high-impact end of the craft: **data systems, field-ready apps and infrastructure** that people actually depend on. My work spans mobile, web and backend, from survey platforms and billing systems to AI-assisted irrigation.

- 🏗️ **Systems Architect** at [Sarhad Rural Support Programme (SRSP)](https://www.srsp.org.pk), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, designing and shipping data infrastructure for development programmes
- 🚀 **Founder & Managing Director** of **Varg Continuum**, an IT services startup building custom software and smart-hardware products
- 🎓 **Lab Instructor** at IMSciences, Peshawar, teaching the next batch of engineers
- 🔬 Currently exploring **IoT, embedded health devices and applied ML** through a self-directed research programme
- 🌱 Always learning, always shipping

> *"Build it simple, build it solid, build it to last."*
